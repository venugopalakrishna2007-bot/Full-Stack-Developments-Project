🏛️ Central & State Welfare Schemes

Central Sector
💰

PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 directly transferred into the Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of landholder farmer families.

Eligibility: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land.
Required Docs: Aadhaar Card, Land Revenue Records, Bank Passbook.
🌐 Visit Official PM-Kisan Portal
Crop Insurance
🛡️

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance covering non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rains, pest epidemics). Uniform low premium: 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi, 5% for Horticulture.

Claim Window: Notify crop damage within 72 hours via Crop Insurance App.
Coverage: Pre-sowing to post-harvest localized calamities.
🌐 Visit Official PMFBY Portal
Soil Health
🧪

Soil Health Card Scheme (SHC)

Provides crop-wise nutrient recommendations for 12 essential soil parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC), preventing indiscriminate fertilizer misuse and reducing cultivation overhead.

Cycle: Issued every 2 years through District Soil Testing Labs.
Benefit: 8-10% average savings on chemical fertilizers.
🌐 Visit Soil Health Portal
Institutional Credit
💳

Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme

Timely credit up to ₹3,00,000 at a subsidized base interest rate of 7%, dropping down to an effective 4% interest rate with timely loan repayment incentives.

Validity: 5-year revolving credit limit with ATM-enabled RuPay card.
Collateral Free: No collateral required for agricultural loans up to ₹1.60 Lakh.
🌐 Learn More on MyScheme
Irrigation Subsidy
💧

Per Drop More Crop (PMKSY)

Micro-irrigation subsidy offering up to 55% subsidy for small/marginal farmers and 45% for other farmers on modern Drip and Sprinkler installations, saving up to 40% water.

Focus: Water conservation, fertigation, and higher farm yield.
Sanction Authority: District Horticulture / Agriculture Officer.
🌐 Visit Official PMKSY Portal
Scientific Advisory
🐛

Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

Ecologically sound pest suppression: combine biological controls (Trichogramma, Neem oil 1500ppm), pheromone traps (5/acre for bollworms), yellow sticky cards, and resort to synthetic sprays only above ETL.

Core Principle: Protect beneficial predators (spiders, ladybird beetles).
Helpline: National Institute of Plant Health Management (NIPHM).
🌐 Visit NIPHM Advisory

🎓 Academic Project Details

Agriculture Digital Support for Farmers

Undergraduate Engineering Capstone / Domain Application

Faculty Mentor Dr. K. Dinesh Academic & Research Supervisor
Team Lead G. Hari Charan ID: 2500040163
Team Member H. Venu Gopal Krishna ID: 2500040237

Technical Specifications

  • Architecture: Single-Page Application (SPA) with zero external frameworks or backend calls.
  • Persistence: Client-side HTML5 Web Storage API (localStorage), zero data egress.
  • Form Validation: Native HTML5 Constraint API combined with JavaScript ES6 RegExp patterns.
  • Internationalization / Currency: Intl.NumberFormat('en-IN') for standard Indian Rupee notation.
  • Usability Standard: Mobile-first fluid responsive grid, WCAG AAA sunlight-legible contrast.
